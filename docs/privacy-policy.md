# Particulars — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 26 October 2026_

Particulars is an app for keeping copies of your vehicle documents (such as your driver's licence, vehicle licence, insurance certificate and roadworthiness certificate) on your phone. This policy explains what happens to the information you put into it.

## What the app stores

When you use Particulars you may enter:

- vehicle details, such as plate number, make, model, year and colour
- the owner's name and phone number
- document numbers, issue dates and expiry dates
- photos of your car and of your documents

## Where it is stored

Everything you enter is stored **only on your own phone**, inside the app's private storage.

- Particulars has no account, no sign-in and no server.
- Nothing you enter is uploaded, sent to us or shared with anyone.
- We cannot see, recover or delete your information, because we never receive it.

If you uninstall the app or clear its data, your information is deleted from the phone and cannot be recovered by us.

## Permissions

- **Camera and photos**: used only when you choose to take or pick a photo of your car or a document.
- **Notifications**: used only to remind you on your phone before a document expires. Reminders are created and shown on your phone; nothing is sent over the internet.

## Analytics and advertising

Particulars does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and does not collect device identifiers.

## Children

Particulars is meant for drivers and vehicle owners. It is not directed at children.

## Not a government app

Particulars is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to the Federal Road Safety Corps (FRSC), the Nigeria Police Force, any Vehicle Inspection Office (VIO), any state licensing authority or any other government agency. A copy in the app does not replace your original documents.
